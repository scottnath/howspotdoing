Recreational

Is it legal for adults to possess cannabis in the state of Nevada?

YES

“Adults 21 years and older can legally possess cannabis in Nevada.” — ccb.nv.gov

Is it legal for adults to buy cannabis in a store in the state of Nevada?

YES

“The only legal way to buy cannabis in Nevada is from a state-licensed retail store.” — ccb.nv.gov

Is it legal for adults to grow cannabis in the state of Nevada?

YES

“Adults 21 and older may grow cannabis at home if there is not a licensed retail store within 25 miles.” — ccb.nv.gov

Is it legal to consume cannabis in parks, on sidewalks, or similar outdoor public spaces in the state of Nevada?

NO

“You cannot use cannabis in any public place.” — ccb.nv.gov

Is it legal for public businesses such as cafes, bars, or conference centers to allow on-site cannabis consumption in the state of Nevada?

YES

“Cannabis may be consumed at a licensed cannabis consumption lounge.” — ccb.nv.gov

Medical

Is it legal for medical marijuana patients to possess cannabis in the state of Nevada?

YES

“Medical cannabis cardholders may possess up to 2.5 ounces of usable cannabis.” — ccb.nv.gov

Is it legal for medical marijuana patients to buy cannabis in a store in the state of Nevada?

YES

“Medical cannabis may be sold to a patient with an active government-issued medical card.” — ccb.nv.gov

Is it legal for medical marijuana patients to grow cannabis in the state of Nevada?

YES

“Medical cannabis cardholders may grow up to 12 plants under certain circumstances, including when they live more than 25 miles from a dispensary.” — ccb.nv.gov
