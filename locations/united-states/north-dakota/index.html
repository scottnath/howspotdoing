Medical

Is it legal for medical marijuana patients to possess cannabis in the state of North Dakota?

YES

“A registered patient with a North Dakota medical marijuana card may possess medical marijuana purchased from a registered dispensary.” — hhs.nd.gov

Is it legal for medical marijuana patients to buy cannabis in a store in the state of North Dakota?

YES

“an individual with a North Dakota medical marijuana card is only authorized to purchase and possess medical marijuana from one of the eight registered dispensaries in the state.” — hhs.nd.gov

Is it legal for medical marijuana patients to grow cannabis in the state of North Dakota?

NO

“Cardholders may possess only medical marijuana purchased from an in-state registered dispensary. State law has no protections for marijuana grown at home.” — hhs.nd.gov
